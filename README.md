# Lois Akara

A small order app for Lois's akara spot: take orders fast, keep the queue, and count the day's sales.

- **New order:** akara and puff-puff by naira amount (tap ₦100 … ₦1,000 or type any amount → quantity and any change left over are worked out) or by quantity; Kunu Gyada by the cup. The total adds itself up. Optional customer name, and payment: cash, transfer or not yet.
- **Queue:** numbered orders (starting at #1 each day) with Ready, Collected and Cancel, and unpaid orders in red.
- **Today:** orders, total sales, cash vs transfer, unpaid, and how much of each item was sold.
- **Transfer card:** shows the bank details and amount to turn toward a customer.
- **Settings:** shop name, prices and bank details; download all orders as CSV.

Works offline and installs on the phone (Add to Home Screen). Orders are stored on the phone only.

Menu: Akara ₦50 · Puff-puff ₦100 · Kunu Gyada ₦300 per cup (prices editable in Settings).
