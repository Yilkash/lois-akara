# Mai Kosai

*Northern Roots. Unforgettable Taste.*

A small order app for Lois's akara (kosai) spot, Mai Kosai: take orders fast, keep the queue, and count the sales. (It started as "Lois Akara"; the web address keeps that name so the installed app keeps working.)

Live: https://yilkash.github.io/lois-akara/

- **Home:** a New order button, money in today (cash + transfer actually paid), with orders not paid yet shown separately, who is waiting, past days, items sold today and today's orders. Tap any order to see it in full or delete it.
- **New order:** tap Akara, Puff-puff or Kunu Gyada. A panel with its own number pad opens:
  - Akara and puff-puff go by naira amount (₦300 → 6 akara, with any change shown) or by pieces.
  - Kunu goes by the cup.
  - Tap the same item again to add a **separate pack**, numbered 1, 2, 3 under the same customer (e.g. two ₦500 akara instead of one ₦1,000). Tap a pack in the order to change or remove it.
  - Add the customer's name (optional) and the payment: cash, transfer or pay later.
- **Queue:** numbered orders (from #1 each day) with Paid, Mark ready, Collected and Cancel. Orders not collected by midnight stay here with a "Yesterday" tag.
- **Past days:** last 7 days or this month, with a bar per day, the best day, and a page for every day.
- **Transfer card:** shows the bank details and amount to turn toward a customer.
- **Settings:**
  - Shop name, dark mode and colour style.
  - Your own menu photos.
  - Prices and bank details.
  - Install the app.
  - Download all orders as CSV, and delete all orders.

Works offline and installs on the phone. Orders are stored on the phone only. When a new version is online, a "New version ready" bar offers to update.

Menu: Akara ₦50 · Puff-puff ₦100 · Kunu Gyada ₦300 per cup (prices editable in Settings).
